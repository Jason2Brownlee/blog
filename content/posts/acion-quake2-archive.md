I've been working on a new Quake archive, this one is for Action Quake II (AQ2).

What is Action Quake2?

> Action Quake2 is a DM mod designed along the lines of a good action movie. No BFGs or rapid fire rocket launchers here, and not a plasma weapon or laser gun in sight. What we DO have is a mod where stealth and strategy pay off more than the the ability to shoot yourself in the feet with a BFG.

I played many many hours of action quake2 back in the day. Later I moved on to Urban Terror for Q3.

You can visit the archive here:

* [Action Quake II Archive](https://github.com/Jason2Brownlee/ActionQuake2Archive)

You can see the reconstructed release history here:

* [Action Quake II Release History](https://github.com/Jason2Brownlee/ActionQuake2Archive/blob/main/research/history.md)

The archive has most of the later releases and many of the important early release. I was surprised, given the popularity of the mod and the era (1998-1999) I expected more of the releases to have been archived by the Wayback Machine.
